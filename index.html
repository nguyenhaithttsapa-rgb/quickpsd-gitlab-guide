Opening Photoshop Files on ChromeOS

Chromebooks and ChromeOS laptops are lightweight, affordable, and deliver all-day battery life. But there has always been one major hurdle for students, photographers, and young creators: Adobe Photoshop does not run natively on ChromeOS.

Step-by-Step Chromebook Instructions:

  1. Launch Google Chrome on your Chromebook.
  2. Navigate to QuickPsd.com.
  3. Click File > Open From Computer or drag your .psd file directly from your Chromebook Files app into the browser.
  4. Edit your project: change typography, reorder layers, and export high-res PNGs to Google Drive.
